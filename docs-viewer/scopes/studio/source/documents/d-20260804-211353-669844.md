Existing structurally valid identities remain associations when their current Catalogue target is unavailable. Canonical mutation formatting quotes digit-only strings, including leading zeroes.
- The configured Projects `authoring_subject` group owns the exact `folder_path`, `work_id`, and `series_id` field set. Strict assignment and import accept at most one subject; Folder retains configured-base normalization, while Work and Series retain exact canonical strings. The existing revision-bound assignment route and exact `{scope, sub_scope, doc_id}` target remain unchanged.
- Private Manage manifests carry normalized subject rows and one deterministic `subject_generation` receipt. `subject-associations.json` groups valid Folder, Work, and Series subjects to zero or more exact document targets and composed current Manage locations under the same receipt. Rebuilds clear removed and final associations without reading live folders, catalogue membership, route selection, titles, filenames, or body content.
- The Projects contribution reads normalized manifest state. Folder uses a folder cue, Work and Series use a catalogue cue with exact accessible labels, None contributes no cue, and invalid/conflicting source contributes an observational warning. Info remains passive. Work and Series choices reuse Catalogue search and picker ownership while filtering out Moment targets.
- Public sub-scope manifests, Manage manifests generated for public-readonly collections, document payloads, and output inventories retain their public-safe schemas. Private subjects, raw invalid evidence, local paths, assignment capability, and association files remain local.
