# Analytics Roadmap

Analytics retirement is complete. Tag management belongs to Studio, document
sharing belongs to integrated Docs Viewer, and tag-package sharing is retired
without replacement.

| suggested order | feature | status | depends on | outcome |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Analytics App Retirement](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260718-155350-d95b37) | done | [Tag Management In Studio](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260718-155350-a2d49c) | Analytics app/runtime removed after final ownership cutover |

Paused Analytics concepts remain available for reference but are not a reason to retain the application. Source-faithful document-package work now belongs under Share Document Packages; future tag assistance belongs under Studio and remains human-mediated.

## Current Position

Document sharing has moved to Docs Viewer, tag-package sharing and the generic
repository Data Sharing subsystem are retired, and Studio owns canonical tags
and all retained tag workflows. There is no future Analytics delivery queue;
new work belongs on the roadmap of its current domain owner.
