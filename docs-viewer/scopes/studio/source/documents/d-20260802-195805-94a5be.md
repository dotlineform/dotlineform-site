# SSP-5 — Work And Series Document Metadata Publication Delivery

## Status And Purpose

SSP-5 is complete. SSP-5.1 ships the exact public-document URL projection and the `work_record_v4` / `series_record_v2` by-ID payload contracts; SSP-5.2 adds post-success Publish follow-through for exact affected payloads with prior-complete-data retention and explicit stale evidence on derived failure. The completed [SSP-5.0 sanity note](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260807-132505-d0570b) confirms the bounded fit, owners, blast radius, deliverables, risks, and stop conditions. The completed SSP-1 foundation supplies optional document-level `work_id`/`series_id` metadata and ordinary publication supplies the public document set. SSP-4 enables the normal `dotlineform/projects` to `analysis/works` editorial workflow but is not a semantic prerequisite for documents published through another valid route. [Sub-Scope Publishing Concept And Architecture](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260802-194358-7717ee) owns the public association and failure boundaries implemented by this delivery.

SSP-5 publishes exact Work/Series document-link metadata without making canonical catalogue records own document associations or adding a browser lookup. Whether and how public Work or Series routes display that metadata belongs to a later Catalogue presentation delivery outside Sub-Scope Publishing.

## Accepted SSP-1 Input

SSP-5 applies the shared SSP-1 reader to each final public-eligible canonical document source and accepts only valid `{kind: "work", key: <five-digit-id>}` or `{kind: "series", key: <lowercase-id>}` state. None, Folder, malformed, and conflicting states retain ordinary publication behavior and contribute the empty Work/Series URL result. Several public documents may share one exact subject.

The private `docs_subject_associations_v1` product, its Manage locations, `subject_generation`, invalid evidence, and row cues remain local inputs for Manage consumers. SSP-5 joins every final public-eligible canonical document source to its existing public document location by exact document identity, preserving exact Work-only or Series-only identity without roll-up, fan-out, title/filename inference, or Catalogue-membership expansion. No separate file represents a complete public document inventory.

## Complete Outcome

Each public Work payload at `site/assets/works/index/<work_id>.json` and Series payload at `site/assets/series/index/<series_id>.json` contains a deterministic `doc_url[]` projection for zero or more public documents whose final canonical source declares that exact valid subject. The source document may belong to any collection participating in ordinary publication. The array contains public document URLs and remains separate from canonical catalogue fields and authored links. SSP-5 leaves the field unconsumed by public route code.

A published Work-subject document appears only in its exact Work payload; a Series-subject document appears only in its exact Series payload. Work membership does not roll the document up to a Series, and Series membership does not fan the document out to member Works. A published document with no usable catalogue subject remains ordinarily published and appears in neither reverse lookup. Publish, unpublish, delete, and published subject changes trigger bounded follow-through for the exact affected old and new Work or Series payloads after the canonical document mutation. Failure never rolls back or repeats that mutation; the prior complete payload remains available with explicit stale evidence where the existing publication workflow supports it.

## Aboutness And Mentions

SSP-5 treats one valid Work or Series subject declaration as explicit aboutness: the author states that the document is about that exact catalogue target. Semantic Tokens have a separate meaning: each occurrence records that the document mentions or relates to a target in its content. A document may mention many Works and Series without being about each of them.

SSP-5 therefore never reads the semantic-token usage index, scans rendered links, or infers `doc_url[]` from content mentions. Adding, removing, or rebuilding a Semantic Token does not change Work/Series document metadata. The SSP-3 **Insert subject link** action may create an ordinary semantic mention of the already-declared subject, but the existing front-matter declaration remains SSP-5's sole association authority.

If documents later need several aboutness subjects, that requires an explicit metadata-model delivery. Semantic mentions must not become an implicit multi-subject shortcut.

## Build Responsibility

The Docs publication/build workflow detects the document's final Work or Series subject and public URL, then schedules updates for the exact affected old and new catalogue targets. The existing Work/Series by-ID generator performs the actual `doc_url[]` write.

- Document publication/build owns detecting and triggering the change.
- Work/Series by-ID generation owns writing the updated payload.
- Project State is not involved.
- Private or non-viewable document builds do not affect public Work/Series links.
- Publish, unpublish, deletion, or subject reassignment updates the relevant old and new targets.

## Delivery Discipline

SSP-5 is a focused public payload projection and publication follow-through delivery. It must not add document links to canonical `works.json` or `series.json`, make catalogue association metadata a publication gate, alter ordinary publication eligibility or placement, publish non-viewable or Manage-only documents, add or change a public browser consumer, infer broader catalogue associations from semantic mentions or rendered links, or turn either payload into a general relationship record.

Curator-facing catalogue document coverage is a separate reporting consumer of SSP-1's private Work/Series associations. Its likely presentation belongs to the Studio Works reporting family and may be scheduled with a later route refactor. Public `doc_url[]` presentation is also deferred: a later Catalogue delivery outside this feature must design the Work and Series experiences deliberately, including whether they share a treatment, where links appear, and how zero, one, or several links behave.

SSP-5.0 is a short scope-and-spec sanity gate over SSP-1's optional metadata projection, final public-eligible canonical sources, existing public document locations, publication follow-through, and Work/Series payload generation. It confirms broad owners, blast radius, deliverables, risks, and stop conditions without freezing exact files, fixtures, estimates, or implementation. Each implementation stage ends with focused evidence and an independent code/diff review before the next stage begins.

## Completion Evidence

- A public document from any collection enters only the exact declared Work or Series payload when its final source has one valid catalogue subject.
- A public document with no subject or unusable subject remains published and enters no Work/Series payload through this projection.
- A semantic-token mention without the matching exact subject declaration never adds the document to Work/Series metadata.
- Several documents may appear for one subject in deterministic order without a scalar or inferred-primary shortcut.
- Work-to-Series roll-up and Series-to-Work fan-out remain absent.
- Non-viewable, unpublished, deleted, unavailable, or reassigned documents have explicit tested behavior.
- Canonical Work/Series source and authored links remain unchanged.
- Work and Series routes and browser consumers remain unchanged and do not display `doc_url[]` in this delivery.
- Targeted follow-through updates exact affected Work/Series payloads and does not repeat its initiating document mutation.

The [Sub-Scope Publishing feature](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260802-194357-4d857b) owns the flat SSP sequence. SSP-5 owns only public Work/Series payload enrichment and exact publication follow-through. A later Catalogue delivery outside this feature owns public presentation.
