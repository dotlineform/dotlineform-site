# SSP-6 — Work And Series Document Metadata Publication Delivery

## Status And Purpose

Proposed as **SSP-6** after SSP-1 projects optional document-level `work_id`/`series_id` metadata and ordinary publication supplies the public document set. SSP-4 enables the normal `dotlineform/projects` to `analysis/works` editorial workflow but is not a semantic prerequisite for documents published through another valid route. [Sub-Scope Publishing Concept And Architecture](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260802-194358-7717ee) owns the public association and failure boundaries implemented by this delivery.

SSP-6 makes published documents discoverable from their exact public Work or Series pages without making canonical catalogue records own document associations or adding another browser lookup.

## Complete Outcome

Each public Work payload at `site/assets/works/index/<work_id>.json` and Series payload at `site/assets/series/index/<series_id>.json` contains a deterministic `doc_url[]` projection for zero or more public documents whose final canonical source declares that exact valid subject. The source document may belong to any collection participating in ordinary publication. The array contains public document URLs and remains separate from canonical catalogue fields and authored links. Work and Series routes present the URLs as distinct related-document metadata through their existing by-ID payload reads.

A published Work-subject document appears only in its exact Work payload; a Series-subject document appears only in its exact Series payload. Work membership does not roll the document up to a Series, and Series membership does not fan the document out to member Works. A published document with no usable catalogue subject remains ordinarily published and appears in neither reverse lookup. Publish, unpublish, delete, and published subject changes trigger bounded follow-through for the exact affected old and new Work or Series payloads after the canonical document mutation. Failure never rolls back or repeats that mutation; the prior complete payload remains available with explicit stale evidence where the existing publication workflow supports it.

## Delivery Discipline

SSP-6 is a focused public projection and existing Work/Series-page consumer. It must not add document links to canonical `works.json` or `series.json`, make catalogue association metadata a publication gate, alter ordinary publication eligibility or placement, publish non-viewable or Manage-only documents, load the association index separately in the browser, infer broader catalogue associations, or turn either payload into a general relationship record.

Curator-facing catalogue document coverage is a separate reporting consumer of SSP-1's private Work/Series associations. Its likely presentation belongs to the Studio Works reporting family and may be scheduled with a later route refactor; SSP-6 remains responsible for public by-ID payload enrichment and public page presentation.

SSP-6.0 is a short scope-and-spec sanity gate over SSP-1's optional metadata projection, the complete public document inventory, publication follow-through, Work/Series payload generation, and catalogue metadata presentation. It confirms broad owners, blast radius, deliverables, risks, and stop conditions without freezing exact files, fixtures, estimates, or implementation. Each implementation stage ends with focused evidence and an independent code/diff review before the next stage begins.

## Delivery Steps

### SSP-6.0 — Confirm Scope And Delivery

- [ ] Sanity-check public Work/Series reverse metadata against the accepted SSP-1 optional-subject contract and current complete public-document inventory, document-location, publication, Work/Series by-ID generation, scoped catalogue rebuild, and metadata presentation models; resolve only contradictions that would change the result.
- [ ] Confirm the broad blast radius across all public collection sources, public association reads, Work and Series payload schema/versioning, existing metadata presentation, publish/unpublish/delete/reassignment impact handling, and public isolation.
- [ ] Confirm the deliverables: deterministic `doc_url[]`, no-subject publication preservation, non-blocking unusable-subject evidence, distinct Work/Series related-document links, exact old/new follow-through, prior-complete-data retention, focused evidence, and concise durable owner updates.
- [ ] Stop for review if the result requires canonical catalogue association ownership, another browser lookup, Analysis lifecycle coupling, non-viewable/Manage publication, subject fan-out, a general relationship payload, or another boundary excluded above. Do not produce an exact file inventory, line estimate, frozen implementation, fixture implementation, or complete test architecture at this gate.

**Checkpoint:** a concise sanity note confirms the spec, SSP-1 dependency, broad owners, blast radius, deliverables, risks, subject-independent publication boundary, and stop condition. Stop before implementation approval.

### SSP-6.1 — Enrich Work And Series By-ID Payloads

- [ ] Add a public-safe reverse-association projection over every published document's final canonical source, independent of collection identity or the route used to publish it.
- [ ] Project deterministic URL-string `doc_url[]` separately from canonical catalogue fields and existing authored links.
- [ ] Recompute each payload version from the complete enriched payload and preserve ordinary zero-document output.
- [ ] Prove no-subject, malformed-subject, and conflicting-subject documents retain ordinary public output while contributing no `doc_url[]`; keep Manage-only paths, local `folder_path`, non-viewable documents, association infrastructure, and Registry data absent from payloads.
- [ ] Prove Work-subject URLs enter only exact Work payloads and Series-subject URLs enter only exact Series payloads.

**Checkpoint:** focused association, payload-schema/version, zero/many ordering, subject isolation, stale/unavailable, targeted-generation, and public-isolation evidence passes. An independent code/diff review confirms `doc_url[]` is a derived public projection separate from canonical Work/Series source and authored links; browsers continue to read their existing by-ID payloads and presentation remains SSP-6.2-owned. Stop before SSP-6.2.

### SSP-6.2 — Present Work And Series Documents

- [ ] Add one distinct related-documents metadata row for zero or more `doc_url[]` links on the existing Work and Series pages using their current components.
- [ ] Preserve current title, catalogue, Series-membership, download, and authored-link presentation.
- [ ] Verify zero, one, and several URLs; deterministic order; stable accessible link labelling; unavailable payloads; keyboard navigation; and narrow layout without adding a new page section or modal.

**Checkpoint:** focused Work/Series renderer/component evidence and representative keyboard/narrow-layout review pass for zero, one, and several document links. An independent code/diff review confirms each existing by-ID payload remains its page's sole browser read and current metadata rows retain their owners and values. Stop before SSP-6.3.

### SSP-6.3 — Add Follow-Through And Verify

- [ ] Rebuild only affected Work or Series payloads after successful publication changes in any collection using exact old/new typed-subject and public-location evidence.
- [ ] Preserve source mutation success and prior complete public data on derived failure; never retry publication or document mutation implicitly.
- [ ] Run focused association, Work/Series payload, publication, subject-isolation, renderer, public-isolation, targeted-rebuild, lint, whitespace, and realistic manual checks.
- [ ] Perform the independent final code/delivery review, reconcile completion claims with exact evidence and omissions, and record any bounded follow-up trigger.
- [ ] Transfer the shipped `doc_url[]` contract to the durable Catalogue payload and Work/Series-page owners.

## Completion Evidence

- A public document from any collection appears only on the exact declared Work or Series page when its final source has one valid catalogue subject.
- A public document with no subject or unusable subject remains published and appears on no Work/Series page through this projection.
- Several documents may appear for one subject in deterministic order without a scalar or inferred-primary shortcut.
- Work-to-Series roll-up and Series-to-Work fan-out remain absent.
- Non-viewable, unpublished, deleted, unavailable, or reassigned documents have explicit tested behavior.
- Canonical Work/Series source and authored links remain unchanged.
- Work and Series routes still perform their existing single by-ID payload fetches.
- Targeted follow-through updates exact affected Work/Series payloads and does not repeat its initiating document mutation.

The [Sub-Scope Publishing feature](/docs/?scope=studio&doc=d-20260802-194357-4d857b) owns the flat SSP sequence. SSP-6 owns only public Work/Series payload enrichment and presentation.
