I’ve drafted **40 words for the new Concepts collection**, with one paragraph per word and links to Catalogue works, Moments, and Library readings that could get each essay started. The headings are alphabetical for review, and each section can become a Concepts document. The set keeps 29 existing headings and proposes 11 additions: **Balance, Constraint, Doll, Dream, Fold, Ghost, Home, Interval, Model, Vessel, and Voice**.

I kept **Ambiguity**, **Clarity**, and **Uncertainty** because they give you different questions to work with. Ambiguity asks how several readings can coexist. Clarity asks what focus or defocus lets you see. Uncertainty asks how much confidence the evidence supports. **Trace** moves between all three. It can be what remains, how little remains, or the act of following what remains to see where it leads.

Several works recur across the draft so you can develop the essays in passes. In *Nerve*, **Doll** asks what we entrust to a figure that cannot answer, **Vessel** asks what giving a feeling a place to live makes possible, and **Voice** asks what happens when we speak for that figure. A discovery in any one of those essays gives you a reason to revisit the other two.

>these are very interesting. there is an existentialist thread running through many of them - what does meaning mean in a world that seems meaningless? why do we need to construct meaning?

I hear that thread most clearly in *will we leave residues*. The poem asks what will remain of a life and whether someone will find a pattern in it. A trace can establish that someone was here. Its significance depends on what someone else is able to see in it, remember through it, or carry forward from it. And there’s no guarantee that anyone will come along to do that. [will we leave residues](/docs/?collection=moments&doc=d-20260215-000000-b24b4f)

There are several senses of *meaning* in your question. You can understand what something says and still wonder why it matters. You can feel that it matters deeply and still be unable to say what it means. I think the existential question is especially about **what can matter in the absence of any guarantee that it matters in some larger scheme**. We need to find or make meaning because we have to decide what deserves our attention, care and time. We also want some of what we experience to become available to other people. Making a work is one way of giving them something they can meet on their own terms.

*We are the vessel* takes that a step further. The search for Plato’s Forms turns into a search for love, or for the shared experience of exploring. There’s a thought there about what making art can do. The search itself can become a thing people share, even while the question that set it in motion stays open. That helps explain why **Ambiguity** matters so much here. The work can give someone a way into the search while leaving them room to find a relation you hadn’t seen. [we are the vessel](/docs/?collection=moments&doc=d-20260215-000000-c27a38)

One question I’d carry through the forty is **what do we do to make something matter, and what happens when it begins to matter to someone else?** *Doll* can ask that through a figure we entrust with feeling. *Trace* can ask it through what survives us. *Hope* can ask it from the middle of an unfinished painting, when the maker still has to decide whether to keep going.

- **Ambiguity** brings in Simone de Beauvoir. Her account of acting within circumstances we didn’t choose opens a question for the work itself. What happens to its meaning when someone else takes it up? [The Ethics of Ambiguity, part I](https://www.marxists.org/reference/subject/ethics/de-beauvoir/ambiguity/ch01.htm)
- **Doll** sets Jung’s account of projection beside *Nerve*. What happens to a fear or desire when it begins to feel like a quality of the figure we’ve given it to? [Psychology and Alchemy, §§345–347](https://www.jungiananalysts.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/C.-G.-Jung-Collected-Works-Volume-12_-Psychology-and-Alchemy.pdf)
- **Trace** takes up the *Khemaka Sutta*’s image of washed cloth that still carries a scent. It gives *will we leave residues* another question to follow. Can what remains shape what happens next even when nobody recognises it as a trace? [Khemaka Sutta](https://www.dhammatalks.org/suttas/SN/SN22_89.html)
- **Vessel** puts Jung’s alchemical vessel beside the *Daodejing*’s vessel, whose empty space makes it useful. That brings two questions to *little clear vessel*. What can happen because something is held inside, and what can happen because space is kept available? [Psychology and Alchemy, §338](https://www.jungiananalysts.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/C.-G.-Jung-Collected-Works-Volume-12_-Psychology-and-Alchemy.pdf) · [Daodejing, chapter 11](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/T%C3%A2o_Teh_King#11)
